    What is this?

    An embedding model has read 1,500 everyday ideas and turned each into a list of numbers that captures its meaning. Ideas with similar meanings get similar numbers, so they sit close together in this 3D cloud: space words in one region, music in another, feelings somewhere else.

    Pick two far-apart ideas. A search algorithm (choose from 16) hunts for the chain of small, sensible steps between them while you watch, and then the shockwave blows everything else away.
